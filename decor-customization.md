collections, and customization options. Work in 3 stages and stop for my approval after each:
Stage A = sections 0–2, Stage B = sections 3–5, Stage C = sections 6–8.

0. AUDIT
- Match the new files to the decor ids below; report missing/extra files and any fringe/background issues.

1. NEW DECOR CATALOG (config-driven, with price, collection, layer, behaviors)
IMPORTANT: NO decor, collection item, or tank style is locked by player level. Everything is
purchasable at any level, limited only by price (shells/pearls). Remove level gating for all
existing decor too, and show no "Unlocks at Lv X" labels for decor or styles.
Layers: "sand" (sits on the sand line), "surface" (floats at the water surface), "back" (behind fish),
"front" (over fish). Sand items can be set to back or front by the player.

Nature: moss_ball (15 shells, gently rolls with currents) · driftwood (60) · flower_plant
(50, sway + flowers glow softly at night) · coral_branch (120, glint) · anemone (150,
tentacles sway via strip warp; clownfish love to hover in it) · sea_fan (130, slow sway) ·
lily_pad (40, surface layer, bobs and casts a soft shadow on the water below)
Ancient Ruins: column (180) · sunken_vase (200, occasional bubble from its mouth) ·
broken_arch (260, fish sometimes swim through the gap) · stone_head (6 pearls, blows a
bubble ring every ~40s)
Cozy Village: bench (60) · mailbox (70, its flag pops up when the daily gift is ready;
tapping it opens the gift) · lantern (90, warm glow at night with a light halo) · tiny_cottage
(300, windows glow at night, bubbles puff from the chimney, a fish occasionally peeks out the door)
Playful: rubber_duck (30, surface layer, bobs and spins slowly) · diver (100, steady bubble
stream from the helmet) · volcano_bubbler (220, calm bubbling with a big bubble burst every ~45s)
· bubble_wall_base (150, code-drawn bubble curtain rising from the bar; fish love swimming through
it) · toy_submarine (5 pearls, surface/mid layer, drifts slowly side to side with a code-drawn
spinning propeller)
Halloween (event, available in October only via local date): pumpkin (80 shells, glows orange at night)
· spooky_tree (120, gently sways). Owned event items stay forever.

2. COLLECTIONS & SET BONUSES
- Placing 3+ items from the same collection in one tank activates its set bonus: +5 happiness for
  fish in that tank, plus a themed ambient effect:
  Nature = drifting pollen-like specks · Ruins = slow sand motes in light rays · Cozy Village =
  firefly-like glowing plankton at night · Playful = occasional rainbow bubbles · Halloween =
  floating mini ghosts at night (cute, not scary).
- Show active set bonuses in the tank's customization panel. Collection progress (owned/total) goes
  in the shop, with a badge when completed.
- Decor happiness now rewards variety: unique decor items give +3 each, and duplicates give +1
  (total decor bonus cap raised to +20).

3. PLACEMENT & EDIT MODE
- A dedicated Decorate mode (🎨 button): fish fade to 50% opacity, decor gets an edit outline, and a
  bottom tray shows your owned-but-unplaced inventory. Drag from the tray into the tank to place it.
- Each placed item: drag to move (sand items slide along the sand line; surface items along the
  surface), plus a small toolbar to flip horizontally, set back/front, choose a size (S/M/L =
  0.8/1.0/1.2), move to inventory, or sell.
- Undo/redo (last 20 actions) and snap guides (subtle center/edge alignment).
- A placed-decor limit per tank of 15 (was 8), +3 per capacity upgrade. Show the count "🪸 9/15".
- Layout presets: save up to 3 layouts per tank and switch between them (items missing from
  inventory are skipped with a note).
- Mobile: long-press to pick up, two-finger tap to undo, large handles, and the tray as a bottom sheet.

4. TANK CUSTOMIZATION (code-drawn, no new images)
A "Tank Style" tab in Decorate mode with live preview:
- Frame skins: Classic Glass, Warm Wood, Bamboo, Retro Chrome, Pastel Pink, Night Neon (glowing
  edges). Rounded frame drawn around the tank with glossy highlights.
- Substrate: Golden Sand (current), White Sand, Black Gravel (with subtle sparkle), Pastel Pebbles,
  Glow Gravel (glows faintly at night). Procedurally generated textures that match the cartoon style.
- Lighting color: Natural, Warm Sunset, Cool Moonlight, Tropical, Soft Pink, plus a custom color picker
  (subtle tint on the water and light rays; works with the day cycle).
- Water clarity/tint: Crystal, Lagoon Blue, Emerald, Twilight.
- Bubbler style: Classic, Bubble Curtain, Heart Bubbles (200 shells), Off.
- Tank nameplate: a small engraved plaque on the frame showing the tank name (editable; also shown in
  the tank switcher).
- Pricing: some options free, others cost shells/pearls; no level requirements (define in config;
  keep at least 2 free options per category). Owned styles are reusable across all tanks.

5. SHOP UPGRADE
- Decor tab gets filters: collection, layer, price, owned/not owned, and a "New" badge for unseen items.
- "Try it" preview: tapping an item shows it as a ghost in your tank where you can position it,
  with Buy & Place / Cancel.
- Tank Style items appear in a "Styles" tab with live preview.

6. SAVE & MIGRATION

- Bump the save version and migrate existing decor to the new format (default layer by type, size M).
  Never lose owned items.

7. PERFORMANCE

8. TESTS & WRAP-UP
