🐟 Fishbowl Break: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Fishbowl Break is a small browser game. We collect as little as possible and never sell or share your data.

Playing as a guest

If you don't sign in, your game (fish, shells, settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to us.

Cloud save (optional)

If you choose "Save progress", you sign in with an email magic link or with Google. We then store:

  • Your email address (and, with Google sign-in, your basic Google profile: name and profile picture), used only to identify your account.
  • Your game save and the time it was last updated, so you can continue on another device.

We only request the email and profile Google scopes. We cannot see your Google password, contacts, files, or anything else in your Google account.

Where it's stored

Accounts and cloud saves are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The game is hosted on Vercel. Your data is not used for advertising, analytics profiles, or AI training, and no third-party trackers run in the game.

Deleting your data

Logging out clears the copy in your browser. To delete your account and cloud save completely, email us at the address below and we'll remove it.
