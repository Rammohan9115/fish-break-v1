🐟 Fishbowl Break: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Fishbowl Break is a small browser game. We collect as little as possible and never sell or share your data.

Playing as a guest

If you don't sign in, your game (fish, shells, settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to us.

Cloud save (optional)

If you choose "Save progress", you sign in with an email magic link (or the 6-digit code in the same email) or with Google. We then store:

  • Your email address (and, with Google sign-in, your basic Google profile: name and profile picture), used only to identify your account.
  • Your game save and the time it was last updated, so you can continue on another device.

We only request the email and profile Google scopes. We cannot see your Google password, contacts, files, or anything else in your Google account.

Where it's stored

Accounts and cloud saves are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The game is hosted on Vercel. Fonts are served from our own site, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts. Your data is not used for advertising, advertising profiles, or AI training.

How long we keep it: your account and cloud save are kept until you delete your account. Guest data lives only on your device until you clear it.

Anonymous usage statistics

When enabled, the game counts what is used (for example feeding, decorating, breeding, buying, or reaching a level) so we can improve it. This uses PostHog (EU cloud). It is anonymous: events carry a random ID stored on your device (not a cookie, and never your email, account, or fish names), no profile is built, and your IP address is not used to identify you. It respects your browser's Do Not Track setting, and you can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy, which also forgets the random ID.

Deleting your data

Logging out clears the copy in your browser. To delete your account, your email address and your cloud save completely, open Settings → Delete my account & data in the game. It happens immediately and can't be undone; the game on your device is kept so you can carry on as a guest. If you can't use the game, email us at the address below and we'll remove it for you.
