species table. Don't change existing species.

| cory | Cory Catfish | 1 | 20 | 25 | 1.6 | 40 | 9 | 3 | sand |
| kuhli_loach | Kuhli Loach | 2 | 25 | 30 | 1.5 | 50 | 9 | 3 | sand |
| crab | Crab | 2 | 35 | 40 | 1.3 | 70 | 10 | 4 | sand |
| hatchetfish | Hatchetfish | 3 | 30 | 30 | 1.7 | 55 | 9 | 4 | surface |
- Each gets 3 color variants via hue shift + a shiny variant, like the other species.
- Cherry shrimp take HALF a capacity slot (2 shrimp = 1 slot). Show capacity with decimals hidden
  (round up for display) and update capacity math + tests.
- All are breedable with the normal rules.

2. ZONES & MOVEMENT (add a "zone" concept to behavior.ts if it doesn't exist)
- Surface (hatchetfish): stays in the top ~15% of the water, quick darting moves along the surface.
  Every 1–3 min, a tiny hop out of the water with a splash + ripple. Uses the strip body wave (small, fast).
- Sand (cory, kuhli, crab): move along the sand planes (reuse the decor/drop depth planes; each
  animal picks a plane and occasionally changes it), passing in front of or behind decor correctly.
  - Cory: short shuffling bursts, nose down "snuffling" the sand with tiny sand puffs; rests often.
    Occasional cute "wink" (one eye closes briefly via the eye overlay).
  - Kuhli Loach: strong, slow S-shaped strip wave (big amplitude along the whole body, not just the
    tail). Sometimes burrows: sinks into the sand until only its head peeks out for 20–60s, then wiggles
    back out with a sand puff.
  - Crab: walks SIDEWAYS (never flips horizontally; it's a front view). Waddle animation: quick small
    steps with a ±4° rock and a tiny vertical bob per step. Pauses and looks around. Can climb onto
    low decor (rocks, driftwood) and sit on top.
- Cherry Shrimp: walks on the sand AND on top of decor surfaces, picking with little body dips; tapping
  it (or a fish swimming close) makes it do a quick backward tail-flick dart, then settle.

3. CRAB CLAWS (sprite animation via split parts, reusing the chest-lid split approach)
- Configurable left/right claw regions + pivot points per crab sprite, with a dev tool to set them by
  clicking (like the eyePos and lid tools).
- Claws rotate independently: idle "snip" every few seconds, raised waving when happy or petted,
  and snapping to the beat in Dance Mode.

4. HELPER ROLES (small, so cleaning still matters)
- Cory: eats pellets that have LANDED on the sand before they dissolve (prevents the overfeeding algae
  penalty). It seeks landed pellets within range and eats max 1 pellet per 10s per cory.
- Cherry Shrimp: each adds +0.1 cleanliness per minute; the total shrimp bonus is capped at +0.4/min
  per tank.
- Crab: every ~15 min, it digs in the sand with a dust puff and finds 1–2 shells (lands on its plane;
  counts toward the existing drop cap).
- Show each role as a one-line "Special:" description in the Shop and FishCard.

5. PETTING & BOND (use the existing bond system)
- Species flavor while petted: cory closes its eyes and wiggles its whiskers; shrimp waves its antennae;
  kuhli curls into a happy spiral; hatchetfish does a little shimmer; crab raises both claws.
- Soulmate signature tricks: cory = sand-snuffle dance with a heart-shaped sand puff; shrimp =
  backflip; kuhli = ties itself into a loose loop-de-loop; hatchetfish = big jump with a rainbow
  splash; crab = claw-clapping dance.

6. LOOK & DEPTH
- Sand dwellers use the depth plane scale/tint (back smaller and faded, front bigger) and soft contact
  shadows. Surface dwellers get a faint reflection shimmer above them on the water surface.
- Reuse the eye overlay system: set eyePos/eyeSize for each sprite (the crab's eyes are on stalks; the
  overlay should sit on the stalk tips).
- Babies: sand dwellers are smaller and bouncier; baby shrimp are very tiny with exaggerated antennae.

7. INTEGRATION
- Shop (Fish tab), FishCard, My Fish, Breeding panel, Fishdex/collections if present, and the dev panel.
- Onboarding: no changes, but new players should see cory/shrimp as affordable early buys.
- Reduced motion: simplified movement, no hops, smaller waves.

8. TESTS & VERIFY
- Unit: configs, half-slot capacity math, cory pellet eating, shrimp cleanliness cap, crab dig timing
  + drop cap, and zone constraints (surface/sand never leave their zone).
- Performance: 60fps with 20 mixed fish + 6 shrimp + 2 crabs.
- Playwright screenshots on desktop and mobile with all 5 species (adult + baby) in one tank, plus a
  manual checklist (burrowing, hop, claw tools, sideways walking, climbing decor).
